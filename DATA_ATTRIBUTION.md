## 2. Scope of Dataset Usage & Repository Contents

**EdgeCRC-AI** evaluates multimodal Microsatellite Instability (`MSI`) versus Microsatellite Stability (`MSS`) prediction on the publicly accessible, de-identified **The Cancer Genome Atlas Colon Adenocarcinoma (`TCGA-COAD`)** Open-Access cohort (`326` patient cases total; `66` locked holdout validation cases).

### 2.1 Raw Datasets Excluded from This Repository
To respect storage discipline and upstream dataset distribution conventions, **no raw patient images or bulk cohort feature archives are redistributed in this Git repository**:
- Raw TCGA-COAD histopathology tile JPEG archives (`~12 GB`) — excluded via `.gitignore`.
- Full `326`-patient extracted feature tensor directory (`clean_tensors/`, `~1.2 GB`) — excluded via `.gitignore`.
- Clinical credentialed databases (such as `MIMIC-III`, which was used solely upstream by Alsentzer et al. during Bio_ClinicalBERT pretraining) — **zero** MIMIC-III records exist or were processed in this project.

## 3. NIH / NCI TCGA Data Use Restrictions & Obligations

All TCGA-COAD records referenced by EdgeCRC-AI belong strictly to the **TCGA Open-Access Data Tier** (`https://www.cancer.gov/ccg/research/genome-sequencing/tcga/using-tcga-data`). Users and downstream researchers must adhere to the following NIH/NCI Genomic Data Sharing (`GDS`) and TCGA Open-Access rules:

1. **Mandatory Attribution:** Any publication, presentation, report, or derivative documentation utilizing these cohort benchmarks or results must include the verbatim TCGA Research Network acknowledgment statement in Section 1 (`https://www.cancer.gov/ccg/research/genome-sequencing/tcga/using-tcga-data/citing`).
2. **Publication Moratorium Status:** Per NCI/NHGRI policy, all original TCGA disease-working-group publication moratoria have been lifted; Open-Access TCGA data may be cited in academic publications without embargo restrictions.
3. **Strict Prohibition on Re-Identification:** Recipients must **never** attempt to identify, re-identify, or contact any individual human donor/patient whose de-identified specimen contributed to the TCGA Research Network, nor link de-identified TCGA barcodes with external personally identifiable datasets.
4. **Non-Endorsement & Investigational Use Only:** Neither the National Cancer Institute (`NCI`), the National Human Genome Research Institute (`NHGRI`), nor the TCGA Research Network endorses EdgeCRC-AI. All cohort artifacts are provided strictly for investigational computational pathology research and are **not** approved for clinical diagnostic use.
